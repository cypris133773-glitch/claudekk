Verifying a report independently

A certificate we publish names the laboratory that issued it and carries that laboratory's own report number, so it can be checked with them directly rather than taken on our word. A report is only meaningful if it was issued for the sample it claims to describe — which is why we publish nothing against a lot until a certificate for that lot exists.

Requesting a retest

If your own assay disagrees with a published certificate, email us the chromatogram and the lot number. We pull a retain sample from the same lot, send it for reanalysis and publish the result whichever way it comes back. Anything that fails is refunded in the coin you paid with.
