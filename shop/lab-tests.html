Verifying a report independently

Reports are issued by and carry the laboratory's own report number. That number can be checked directly with the lab rather than taken on our word — a report is only meaningful if it was issued for the sample it claims to describe.

Requesting a retest

If your own assay disagrees with the report for a lot, email us the chromatogram and the batch number. We pull a retain sample from the same lot, send it for reanalysis and publish the result whichever way it comes back. Anything that fails is refunded in the coin you paid with.
