How to pay with crypto

We take Bitcoin, Solana and Ethereum — nothing else. There are no cards, no PayPal and no bank transfers, which means no payment processor sits between you and the order and no card data exists to be leaked.

The short version

  1. Add items to the cart and enter a shipping address at checkout.
  2. Pick BTC, SOL or ETH. We quote the exact coin amount and lock it for 30 minutes.
  3. Send that exact amount, in one transaction, to the address shown.
  4. Press I have sent the payment so your transfer can be matched to your order.
  5. We dispatch on confirmation and email tracking the same day.

If something goes wrong

I sent slightly too little
Network fees sometimes come out of the amount rather than on top of it. We credit whatever arrives; if the shortfall is under 2% we ship anyway, and above that we email you with the difference to top up.
I sent too much
Tell us and we either refund the excess to an address you give us, or hold it as credit against your next order — your choice.
I sent on the wrong network
Email us immediately with the transaction ID. Recovery depends on the network and the asset, and it is not always possible — this is why the checkout page names the network for each coin.
The quote expired before I sent
Nothing is lost. Reload checkout for a fresh quote, or send the old amount and we will settle the difference at the rate when the transfer confirms.
The rate moved between quote and confirmation
The USD total is what you owe. We absorb normal movement between the quote and confirmation — you only hear from us if a transfer lands far below the order total.
I want a refund
Refunds go back in the coin you paid with, to an address you nominate, at the rate on the day the refund is sent. See the terms of sale for what is refundable.
We will never email you a different payment address or ask you to re-send a payment to a "corrected" wallet. If you get a message like that, it is not from us — check the addresses on this page.
