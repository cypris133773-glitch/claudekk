Privacy policy

Last updated:

What we collect

  • Order details — the email address, name and shipping address you enter at checkout, and what you ordered.
  • Payment references — the receiving address, amount and, where you send it to us, the transaction ID.
  • Correspondence — emails you send us and our replies.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • Card numbers or bank details — we take crypto only, so they never exist.
  • Accounts and passwords — checkout works without registration.
  • Advertising or cross-site tracking identifiers. There are no third-party trackers or ad pixels on this site.

Storage in your browser

Your cart, your theme preference, the research-use confirmation and a copy of your recent orders are stored in this browser's local storage so the site works across page loads. That data stays on your device — clearing site data removes it, and with it your local order history.

How long we keep order records

Order records are retained for 90 days so we can handle reships, refunds and support, and are then deleted. Where a longer period is required by law for accounting purposes, only the minimum record is kept.

Who we share with

The carrier receives the name and address needed to deliver your parcel. Nobody else receives your data, and we do not sell or rent it. We disclose data only where compelled by a valid legal order.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be erased earlier than the retention period — email privacy@cryptopeptides.com. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Order correspondence is held in a mailbox with two-factor authentication, and access to order data is limited to staff who fulfil and support orders.

This policy is a template and is not legal advice. Have it reviewed against the privacy law that applies to you — GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and others impose specific disclosure requirements.
