Dispatch

Orders confirmed before 2pm on a working day are packed and handed over the same day. Anything later goes out the next working day. Bitcoin payments occasionally take an hour to confirm, which can push an order to the next dispatch window.

Cold-chain packing

Vials travel in a foam-lined insulated mailer with a frozen gel pack. Lyophilised peptides are stable at ambient temperature for the length of a normal shipment, so a thawed gel pack on arrival is not a problem — the cold chain is margin, not a requirement.

Discretion

Plain outer packaging, no product names on the outside, and a neutral sender name. Customs declarations name the contents as laboratory reagents at their declared value, because falsifying a declaration is not something we will do for an order.

Customs and import

Import rules for research chemicals vary by country and are your responsibility as the importer. Duties or import VAT, where they apply, are payable by you. We ship to the address you give us and cannot advise on whether a given compound may be imported into your jurisdiction.

Reship guarantee

If tracking shows no movement for ten business days, or the parcel is confirmed lost or seized, we reship once at our cost or refund you in the coin you paid with. The guarantee covers one occurrence per address; after that we will still work with you, but reshipping repeatedly into the same route is not something we can absorb.

We cannot ship to a jurisdiction where the compound you have ordered is controlled. If that applies to your order we will email you and refund it in full before dispatch.
