About

Peptides for research, sold the way we would want to buy them.

We started because ordering research material was needlessly opaque: reports that never matched the vial in your hand, prices that moved depending on which coupon you found, and checkouts that wanted more personal data than the order needed. We fixed the three things that actually bothered us.

One price

Every vial sits 15% below list, permanently. No codes, no flash sales, no fake timers.

One record per lot

The lot number on the label matches a public record, and its certificate is published there once issued. Nothing is "available on request".

No payment data

Crypto only. We never hold card numbers because we never take them.

How material is sourced

Peptides are made by solid-phase synthesis, purified by preparative RP-HPLC, lyophilised and sealed under nitrogen in amber vials. We buy from a small number of manufacturers we have worked with repeatedly rather than whoever is cheapest that month, and we hold retain samples from every lot so a disputed result can always be re-tested against the same material.

How it is tested

A sample from each lot goes to an independent analytical laboratory. We publish what comes back — including the occasions when a lot fails and is destroyed rather than sold. Until a certificate exists for a lot, its page says so plainly instead of showing a number. A supplier that has never published a bad result is either very lucky or not publishing everything.

How it ships

Orders are packed in insulated mailers with a gel pack, in plain outer packaging with no product names on the label. We aim to get in-stock orders out the same working day once payment is confirmed, and we send the tracking number as soon as the carrier scans the parcel.

What we will not do

We do not give dosing advice, we do not sell to anyone who tells us the material is for personal use, and we do not describe these compounds as treatments. Everything here is supplied for laboratory research, and that is the only basis on which we sell it.

Questions before you order?

The lab desk answers technical questions about purity, batches and handling, usually within a few hours.
