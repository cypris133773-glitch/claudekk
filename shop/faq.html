Frequently asked questions

Ordering & prices

Is the 15% discount really always on?
Yes. The list price is struck through next to the price you pay on every product page, and the discount is applied site-wide. There is no code to enter and no expiry date. On a handful of low-priced consumables rounding to the nearest cent lands a fraction over 15%, never under.
Do I need an account?
No. Checkout takes an email and a shipping address, and nothing else. The order is stored in your browser, so keep the order number — emailing it to us with your transaction ID is how we match your payment.
Is there a minimum order?
No minimum. Orders over $150 ship free to most destinations; below that, tracked shipping runs $14.95–$29.95 depending on where it is going. The shipping page has the table.
Can I change an order after paying?
If it has not shipped, yes — email us with the order number. Once tracking is issued we cannot alter the parcel, but you can refuse delivery and we will refund on return.

Payment

Which coins do you accept?
Bitcoin, Solana and Ethereum. Checkout names the network, shows the receiving address with a QR code of it, and works out the amount from your USD total. Full detail is in the payment guide.
Why crypto only?
Card processors will not serve this category reliably, and every processor that would is another party holding your data. Taking coin directly means no card numbers exist to be stolen and no third party can freeze an order mid-flight.
How long is the quoted rate held?
While we are quoting from a live rate feed, thirty minutes — the countdown is shown, and when it runs out the payment button is disabled until you take a fresh quote. Without a live feed the coin figure is marked approximate and the USD total is what you owe, so there is nothing to expire.
What if I send the wrong amount?
Shortfalls under 2% are absorbed and the order ships. Larger gaps get an email with the difference. Overpayments are refunded or held as credit, whichever you prefer. The same 2% tolerance is the one quoted everywhere else on the site.

Testing & quality

Who tests the material?
An independent analytical laboratory, not us. A published certificate names the laboratory that issued it and carries that laboratory's own report number, so you can check it with them rather than taking our word for it. Where no certificate has been issued for a lot yet, the lot page says exactly that rather than showing a figure. See the lot registry.
How do I match a vial to its report?
Every vial label carries a lot number. Search it in the lot registry and you get that lot's record — when it was filled, and its certificate once one has been issued.
My own assay disagrees with your report.
Send us the chromatogram and lot number. We pull the retain sample for that lot, send it for reanalysis and publish the result either way. If it fails, you are refunded in the coin you paid with.
What purity should I expect?
We specify 99% or better by RP-HPLC for peptides; the figure on a product page is that target, and the assayed figure for the lot you receive comes from its certificate. A handful of larger or more difficult sequences are specified slightly lower and are listed accordingly.

Shipping

How fast do orders go out?
We aim to dispatch in-stock orders the same working day once payment is confirmed. Domestic delivery is typically 2–4 days, international 5–12 depending on customs.
Is the packaging discreet?
Yes. Plain outer packaging, no product names on the label, and a generic sender name on the customs declaration where one is required.
What if my parcel is lost or seized?
Anything that has not moved for ten business days is reshipped once at our cost, or refunded. Seizure notices are covered the same way for a first occurrence.

Handling & storage

How should lyophilised vials be stored?
At −20 °C, protected from light, for long-term storage. Brief ambient exposure in transit does not degrade lyophilised material, which is why cold packs are for margin rather than necessity.
My vial arrived warm — is it ruined?
Almost certainly not. Lyophilised peptides are stable at ambient temperature for the length of a normal shipment. If the cake is discoloured or liquid on arrival, photograph it and email us.

Legal

Can I buy these for personal use?
No. Everything here is supplied for laboratory research only. These are not medicines, they are not approved for human or veterinary use, and we do not sell to anyone intending to consume or administer them.
Will you give me a protocol or dose?
No. We answer questions about purity, handling, solubility and storage. We do not advise on administration.
